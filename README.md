# operational-relief-playbooks

Service scoping blueprints and workflow patterns for solopreneurs and 1–5 person owner-led businesses — from High Capacity AI.

## What lives here
Sanitized before/after workflow maps, anonymized structural patterns, onboarding checklists for owner-led businesses, and scope-boundary definitions.

## What never lives here
Client names (without written release), raw client inputs or outputs, internal automation scripts, or pricing calculators.
